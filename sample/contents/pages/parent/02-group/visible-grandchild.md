## A group without a landing page

There is no page at `parent/group`, so Group is a label rather than a link.
This visible descendant keeps the label in the navigation beneath
[Parent](parent). Its reading-order neighbors are [Child](parent/child)
and [Child 2](parent/child-2).

Its sibling, [Hidden Grandchild](parent/group/hidden-grandchild), still
generates a page but does not appear in the navigation sequence.
